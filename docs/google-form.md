# Booking form: draft questions

Halyna creates these forms in **her own Google account**, so the answers go to her.
Paste the share links (`https://forms.gle/…`) into the site in place of
`[GOOGLE_FORM_UA_URL]` and `[GOOGLE_FORM_EN_URL]`.

Recommended: one form per language. Each site links to its own form, and the
questions stay short.

## Form settings

- **Collect email addresses:** off. Students are contacted through the Instagram or
  Telegram username they give, so the form collects less personal data.
- **Limit to 1 response:** off. It would force students to sign in to Google.
- **Responses:** link to a Google Sheet, and turn on email notifications for new
  responses.
- **Confirmation message:**
  - UA: «Дякую! Я напишу вам в Instagram або Telegram.»
  - EN: "Thank you! I'll message you on Instagram or Telegram."
